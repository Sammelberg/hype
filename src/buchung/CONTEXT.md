# Buchung

How Hype Agency sells a visit to a children's party: who comes, for how long, where, and when the performers are taken.

## Language

### Catalogue

**Figur**:
A character a guest can book, under a descriptive name of our own (Eisprinzessin, Superheld in Rot).
_Avoid_: Charakter, Kostüm, Rolle, any licensed name

**Darsteller**:
A person who plays one or more Figuren and is sent to the party.
_Avoid_: Animateur, Schauspieler, Mitarbeiter

**Paket**:
How long a visit lasts and what happens during it (Mini, Klassik, Premium, Kita/Event), independent of the Figur.
_Avoid_: Angebot, Tarif, Programm

**Programm**:
The list of what a Figur does at the party (games, contests, a show block).
_Avoid_: Ablauf, Leistungen

**Extra**:
Something added to a Buchung: a second Figur, face paint, a candle or flower arrangement, balloons, a photographer.
_Avoid_: Add-on, Zusatzleistung, Option

**Vorlauf**:
The number of days ahead an Extra must be ordered; too late, and it is not offered at all.
_Avoid_: Lieferzeit, Frist

### Places and price

**Zone**:
A ring of distance from Aschaffenburg (A–E) with its own surcharge and travel time.
_Avoid_: Gebiet, Region, Entfernungsstufe

**Stadt**:
A town the customer picks from the list; it belongs to one Zone and may have its own landing page.
_Avoid_: Ort (for the list entry), Standort

**Anzahlung**:
The share of the total paid when booking; it turns a held slot into a confirmed Buchung.
_Avoid_: Vorkasse, Kaution, Deposit

### Booking and time

**Buchung**:
One agreed visit: Figur(en), Paket, Extras, Stadt, start time, contact and price.
_Avoid_: Auftrag, Bestellung, Reservierung, Termin

**Anfrage**:
A Buchung that cannot confirm itself (less than 48 hours ahead, Zone E, outside the Zonen, an on-request Paket or Extra) and waits for the owner.
_Avoid_: Kontaktanfrage (that is the contact form)

**Halten**:
A Buchung's slot is held for a short time while the Anzahlung is being paid; unpaid, it lapses (verfallen).
_Avoid_: Reservieren, Blocken

**Belegung**:
A stretch of time a Darsteller is taken: an Einsatz or a Sperre.
_Avoid_: Termin, Slot, Block

**Einsatz**:
The Belegung of one Darsteller for one Buchung.
_Avoid_: Auftritt, Job

**Sperre**:
A Belegung without a Buchung: holiday, illness, a private appointment.
_Avoid_: Abwesenheit, Blocker, Urlaub (as the general term)

**Fenster**:
The whole time an Einsatz takes: travel there, setting up, the party with its Extras, packing, travel back.
_Avoid_: Dauer (that is the Paket's), Slot

**Beginn**:
The moment the party itself starts, as agreed with the customer; the Fenster opens earlier.
_Avoid_: Ankunft, Startzeit des Fensters

**Storno**:
The cancellation of a Buchung, by the customer or by the agency, with the fee the cancellation grid sets.
_Avoid_: Absage, Rücktritt, Widerruf (that is the statutory right, a different thing)
